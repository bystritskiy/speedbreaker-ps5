# SpeedBreaker for PS5: changelog

What changed in each release of SpeedBreaker for PS5, newest first. The app shows the newest
entry once, the first time you start it after an update.

## 01.000.004

### What's new

- **European discs:** besides the USA disc, the installer now takes the European English disc
  (Need for Speed - Most Wanted (Europe)) and the Spanish one (Spain), each with its own movies
  and speech. It tells them apart by their files; their saves are kept apart from the USA's.
- **Language page** (Settings > Language): Text sets the menus and race text to any of the
  game's ten languages, on any disc, from the next start. Audio shows the language of the
  disc's speech and movies, which only the disc decides.

## 01.000.003

### What's new

Now on SpeedBreaker v0.2.4 (was v0.2.0), with what it brings to the PS5:

- **Sharper UI** (Settings > Graphics, on by default): the menus, the HUD and their text are
  enlarged to 4K with a sharper filter instead of a blur.
- **Sharper movies:** the intro and the cutscenes are enlarged the same way (Movie Upscaling).
- **Sharper shadows:** Shadow Resolution's new Auto, the default, draws the sun's shadows at 2x
  at 4K. A 1x from an earlier version becomes Auto once; choose 1x again to keep it.
- **Cops in Quick Race:** a Cops row in Custom Race's race options (Off by default) puts the
  police in Circuit, Sprint, Lap Knockout and Speedtrap races, in single player.
- **Player 1 follows the DualSense you last pressed,** outside split screen.
- **Cooler Colors** (Settings > Display, off by default): less of the game's yellow.

### More about it

- HDR Output isn't offered on the PS5: the app's picture is SDR.

## 01.000.002

### What's new

- **Two-player split screen** (Quick Race > Split Screen): each player gets their own half of the
  screen. The second DualSense has to be signed in as a second PS5 user; a guest will do. It
  joins at "Player 2 press START" with Options.

## 01.000.001

### What's new

- **Install from the disc image.** Copy the .iso you made from your own disc to
  /data/speedbreaker/ and press Install: every file is checked against the USA disc in under a
  minute, and nothing is copied. The game is played from the image, so keep it where it is.
- **The installer works on a clean console.** Install could be greyed out on a first start, and
  an install closed halfway blocked the next one.
- **Free space** is no longer shown as a made-up 16 GB.

## 01.000.000

### What's new

- **The first PS5 release**, on SpeedBreaker v0.2.0: Need for Speed: Most Wanted's Xbox 360
  version, recompiled to run natively on a homebrew-enabled PS5.
- **Native 4K**: the 3D scene renders at 3840x2160, with 16x anisotropic filtering, at up to
  60 fps.
- **The DualSense throughout**: its button prompts in the menus and races, its diagram on the
  Controls and loading screens, the PlayStation names in the menus, and rumble.
- **SpeedBreaker's settings** open with Touchpad + Options, and Save Bug Report puts the logs and
  a screenshot in one zip in /data/speedbreaker/.

### More about it

- The USA disc only, for now. Your saves, settings and game files are in /data/speedbreaker/.
